# Security policy

Report a vulnerability in any Rainkernel repository **privately**: use *Report a vulnerability* under the repository's Security tab (private vulnerability reporting is enabled), or e-mail security@rainkernel.com. Please do not open a public issue for a security problem.

- We acknowledge a report within two working days and keep you informed until it is resolved.
- We aim to ship a fix, or publish an advisory with a workaround, within 30 days of confirming the issue.
- We credit reporters in the advisory unless you ask us not to.

Supported versions: the latest tagged release of each repository. Our disclosure policy and contact details: https://rainkernel.com/security and https://rainkernel.com/.well-known/security.txt.
