# Code of conduct

Rainkernel's open-source projects are run by a small company that wants them to be useful and pleasant to work on. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, is expected to:

- be courteous and assume good faith; disagree with the idea, not the person;
- keep the discussion about the work: the finding, the test, the evidence;
- accept that the maintainers are few and an answer may take a working day or two;
- never harass, demean or discriminate against anyone, and never share someone else's private information.

Reports go to hello@rainkernel.com (security matters: security@rainkernel.com). A founder reads every report and replies within two working days. We may edit or remove content, lock threads, or block accounts that break these rules. This applies to every Rainkernel repository and to any place where someone is representing a Rainkernel project.
